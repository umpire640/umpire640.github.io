🔧 How to Add Your 3D Models

  1. Convert: Use gltf-pipeline.dev (free) to convert OBJ/FBX/STEP to .glb
  2. Upload: Place .glb files in assets/models/
  3. Update: Change src="assets/models/your-model.glb" in the model-viewer tag
  4. Test: The first model already works with a free sample!
