How to Add Your 3D Models

  1. Convert your models: Use gltf-pipeline.dev to convert OBJ, FBX, STEP, or other formats to GLB (recommended) or GLTF.
  2. Upload to GitHub: Place your .glb files in the assets/models/ folder.
  3. Update the HTML: Uncomment and update the <model-viewer> tags above with your model paths.
  4. Add the script: In your final deployed HTML, add this before </body>: 
    <script src="https://ajax.googleapis.com/ajax/libs/model-viewer/3.0.1/model-viewer.min.js"></script>
  5. Free test model: Try it with src="https://modelviewer.dev/shared-assets/models/Astronaut.glb"

Note: The model-viewer script is loaded from Google's CDN and works on GitHub Pages. It may not load in the canvas preview, but will work when you deploy to your GitHub Pages site.
